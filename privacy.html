Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 12, 2026

English 中文

At Artligent, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our products — currently VSep and BeingTime.

Information We Do Not Collect

Our apps are designed with privacy as a core principle. We do not collect, store, or transmit any audio files, personal data, or usage information to our servers. There are no servers — everything stays on your device.

  • Audio Files (VSep): All audio processing in VSep happens locally on your device using CoreML. Your music never leaves your device and is never uploaded anywhere.
  • Personal Inputs (BeingTime): BeingTime stores only the details needed for your countdown — your birth date, region, gender, and life-expectancy preference — and only on your device. There is no account and no upload.
  • Personal Information: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • Usage Data: We do not track how you use our apps, which features you use, or any other usage statistics.

Local Processing

VSep uses on-device machine learning (Apple's CoreML framework) to separate vocals from music. The AI model is downloaded with the app and runs entirely on your device. No internet connection is required for processing, and no data is sent to external servers.

BeingTime works fully offline. Its life-expectancy data and quote collection are bundled with the app and run entirely on your device. Your inputs are saved locally using iOS on-device storage. No internet connection is required.

In-App Purchases

Some of our apps offer in-app purchases for premium features. These transactions are processed through Apple's App Store infrastructure, and we do not have access to your payment information. Apple may share limited purchase confirmation data with us to verify your purchase or subscription status. BeingTime is completely free with no in-app purchases.

App Store Data

When you download our apps from the App Store, Apple may collect certain information as described in their privacy policy. This includes device information and download statistics. We receive anonymized, aggregated data from Apple about app downloads and crashes, which helps us improve our products.

Third-Party Services

Our apps do not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

Data Storage

All data created by our apps — such as VSep's separated audio tracks, or BeingTime's settings — is stored locally on your device. You have full control over this data and can delete it at any time through the app or by deleting the app entirely.

Children's Privacy

Our apps are not intended for children under 13 years of age. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
